## 2. First-run path, from the code

1. Open flight-player.html. Theme and playback state load from localStorage (lines 165-190). The map shows the first leg, BLR to PNQ.
2. The only flights are the two hardcoded in LEGS (line 128). They carry one person's seat numbers and Hinglish notes.
3. Tap play. Time `t` advances on the wall clock from the tap (loop, line 395). Altitude and phase are fixed fractions of elapsed time: alt(p) and phase(p) at lines 188-189 (cruise at 36,000 ft from 18 percent to 78 percent of the leg).
4. Settings lets you import a flight as JSON (validateLeg, line 2405). It saves to localStorage "flightradar.flights" and nothing reads it back into the map (header comment, line 2273: "Not yet consumed ... hardcoded LEGS").
5. No Barometer, accelerometer or heading is read at any point. Grep for V2_SENSORS finds only its definition and comments (lines 738, 747, 1084).

Friction, with evidence:
- A second user, or the same user on the next trip, sees trips that are not theirs and cannot add one that plays (lines 128, 2273).
- A delayed departure is not handled: the timer starts when the user taps, with no correction. A takeoff detector exists (sensors, lines 866-875) and nothing reads it.
- The README sells "no GPS, sensor fusion"; the player runs on a timer alone.

## 3. Findings, ranked

F1 (high). The phase detector in core does not survive a real cabin.
Evidence. FlightPhaseDetector uses fixed thresholds: climb above 2.0 m/s and descent below minus 2.0 m/s (AltitudeEstimator.kt:60-65, 77, 91); level means under 0.75 m/s (84, 99). A phone sees cabin pressure, not outside pressure. Airliner cabin altitude is held at 8,000 ft or less and its rate is limited to about 500 ft/min up and 300 ft/min down (Wikipedia, Cabin pressurization; patent US5273486A). 300 ft/min is 1.52 m/s, under the 2.0 threshold. The filter blends with a fixed 0.98 per step (AltitudeEstimator.kt:43, 45), so its behaviour depends on the sample rate, but VerifyMain.kt tests only dt = 1 s (lines 52-76). Pressure is not smoothed before differencing (line 44); the web port does smooth it (sensors, 0.25 EMA, line 820).

Measured 25 Nov (python port of the exact filter and detector, synthetic trace, scratchpad tools/sim.py). Assumptions: cabin climbs 500 ft/min to 7,000 ft, holds 90 min, descends at the stated rate, zero accelerometer input, pressure noise 0.05 hPa where stated (my assumption for a phone sensor, to be replaced by a measured value):
- Descent at 200 ft/min, no noise: DESCENT is never detected (1 Hz and 5 Hz).
- Descent at 300 ft/min, no noise: DESCENT fires 16 s after the cabin starts down, caused by filter overshoot, not by the 2.0 m/s threshold. At 50 Hz, no noise: CRUISE is reached and DESCENT never fires.
- With noise 0.05 hPa, 20 seeds each: at 1 Hz a false DESCENT during cruise in 14 of 20 runs; at 5 Hz 1 of 20 runs has a wrong sequence; at 50 Hz 20 of 20 are wrong (CLIMB is never detected).
This is a synthetic result. No real traces exist in the repo (section 6).

F2 (high). Flights you add never play. Evidence: flight-player.html:128, 2273, 2392-2400.

F3 (medium). The sensor layer is dead code in the player, and the browser cannot supply a barometer. Evidence: no callers of V2_SENSORS (grep: lines 738, 747, 1084 only). `'Barometer' in global` (line 814) is false in current browsers: Chromium's generic sensor README says there is no backend for pressure sensors, and the W3C roadmap lists the barometer as discontinued. Real pressure needs a native Android bridge. None exists in this repo; "versionName 1.0 hardcoded" in phone/TASKS.md:391 suggests a wrapper exists somewhere I did not find.

F4 (low, not in top 3). No CI. VerifyMain.kt is not JUnit (README says so). The public README says the app is "not built" while the player exists privately.
F5 (low). The player validates India bounds only (IN_LA_MIN..IN_LO_MAX in the settings block), so a non-India trip cannot be imported.
F6 (risk, not a change). The player file holds one person's itinerary and seat numbers. Anything moved into this public repo must use synthetic flights.

## 4. Top 3 changes

Decision for him first (D1): the player lives in the private cnc repo and carries his itinerary. This plan puts new, testable JS modules and synthetic flights in this public repo under `player/`, and leaves phone/flight-player.html untouched. Merging the modules into the player stays his hand step. Moving the whole player into this repo is a separate decision and needs a scrubbed copy.

### Change 1. Phase detection that works with cabin pressure and any sample rate
User notices: "descent started" and "landing soon" callouts fire at the right time and not in the middle of a calm cruise. In the synthetic test today they fire wrongly or not at all.
Evidence: F1; AltitudeEstimator.kt:43-45, 60-65, 77, 91.
Approach: (a) make the filter weight depend on dt (a time constant in seconds) and smooth pressure before differencing; (b) set thresholds from the cabin-rate numbers (climb above about 1.0 m/s, descent below about minus 0.8 m/s, sustained 20 s or more), exposed as parameters; (c) the same logic in a small JS module `player/phase.js`. The predictor is unchanged.
Expected effect: on the synthetic suite, correct sequence CLIMB, CRUISE, DESCENT, LANDED in 20 of 20 seeds at 1, 5 and 50 Hz, false DESCENT in cruise 0 of 20, each detected within 60 s of the true transition. Today: 0 of 20 correct at 50 Hz, 14 of 20 false at 1 Hz.
Proof: the synthetic generator is committed as a test helper (no real data); run as JUnit on the Kotlin side and `node --test` on the JS side; a before and after table in the PR. Kotlin runs only in GitHub Actions. Step one before any fix: commit the failing tests and show them red in CI.
Risk: thresholds tuned on a synthetic cabin may not hold on a real plane. Mitigation: parameters, plus recording a real trace on his next flight. The cabin-rate figures come from references, not from a measurement.

### Change 2. Flights you add actually play
User notices: add a trip in Settings (or paste JSON) and a new tab appears next to the built-in ones and plays on the map. Delete it and it goes.
Evidence: F2; validateLeg already exists (line 2405) and the storage key exists (line 2392).
Approach: `player/flights.js`, a pure function that merges built-in and saved legs into the LEGS shape; relax the India-only bounds to world bounds with a clear message. Wiring it into the player is a short glue step in his own copy.
Expected effect: a saved flight appears in the tab list after reload: 0 today, 1 after. A non-India leg is accepted.
Proof: `node --test` with a fake localStorage and synthetic legs: add, reload, tab count, delete, corrupt JSON, duplicate id, waypoints out of order. A Playwright run in CI on a scrubbed copy takes screenshots of the new tab at desktop and 360 px width.
Risk: low. The merge is additive: built-in legs keep their index and id, so saved playback state (flightradar.v1, field li) still points at the same leg.

### Change 3. Sensor layer you can see working: replay mode, a source badge, and delay correction
User notices: the player says where its numbers come from ("timer", "recorded trace", "device"). When a trace says the plane took off 17 minutes late, the timeline shifts to match instead of staying wrong.
Evidence: F3; the takeoff detector (sensors, lines 866-875) and read() are never used.
Approach: `player/sensors-replay.js`, a feed that replays a synthetic or recorded trace into the fusion in the same input shape the device would give, plus a pure function `correctTimeline(plannedTakeoffT, detectedTakeoffT)`. The real source (native Android pressure and accelerometer through a JS bridge) is out of scope and stays a documented extension point.
Expected effect: with a synthetic trace that takes off 17 minutes late, timeline error at later waypoints goes from 17 minutes to under 1 minute.
Proof: `node --test` on the correction with synthetic traces: late, early, no takeoff, sensor drop-out falls back to the timer; the badge text asserted per source.
Risk: the descent half depends on Change 1. Takeoff detection by accelerometer in a pocket versus a hand is unproven (2.0 m/s^2 for 4 s, sensors line 750, is a guess), so it is unmeasured until a real trace exists.

Order: 1, then 3. Change 2 is independent and can run in parallel with 1.

### Review round 1 (a fresh read-only reviewer, product and code lens)
Fixed: a long sample gap could flip the phase (now re-seeds and caps the timer step at 5 s); landing never ended when the destination sat 400 m or more above or below the origin (now level after a descent of at least 100 m); a roll with gaps, braking, or an hours-off detection could shift the timeline (now rejected); the badge said "recorded trace" before anything was confirmed and the shift snapped back on a dropout (now "waiting for takeoff", and "timer (sensor lost)" keeps the last shift); ids like "constructor" were rejected as duplicates.
Not fixed, on purpose, listed for later: phases never go backwards (a go-around or a step climb cannot return to CLIMB); a mid-descent level-off after 100 m of descent counts as landed; dep strings are not cross-checked against depH and depM; the "device" source is a label only.
